Kelp forests are large patches of [kelp](../blocks/kelp.md) that generate in the ocean.



## Generation
Kelp forests generate in all ocean regions, _except_ in [frozen oceans](../generation/frozen-ocean.md) and in [coral reefs](../generation/coral-reefs.md).

As per their name, kelp forests are closely interconnected, usually spanning multiple chunks in waving, tendril-like patterns.

Kelp reaches between 2 to 17 blocks in height, frequently near or touching the ocean's surface.
