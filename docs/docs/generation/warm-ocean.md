Warm oceans are large, sandy regions of ocean that can feature [coral reefs](../generation/coral-reefs.md) near their center.

## Generation
Like all ocean regions, [shipwrecks](../generation/shipwreck.md) and ocean monuments generate. [Kelp forests](../generation/kelp-forests.md) do generate, but not within coral reefs.

### Seafloor
<img align="right" img src='https://i.imgur.com/hUbj8jg.png' width='300'>

Warm ocean seafloors feature patches of seagrass, kelp forests, and patches of [sea pickles](../blocks/sea-pickle.md). The seabed is primarily sand.


### Coral reefs
_Main page: [Coral Reefs](../generation/coral-reefs.md)_

Coral reefs are dense clusters of coral that generates on the seafloor of warm oceans. Kelp forests do not generate within it.
