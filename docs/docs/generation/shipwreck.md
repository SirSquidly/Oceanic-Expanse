A shipwreck is a broken ship structure found in any ocean biomes.

## Generation
Shipwrecks generate in all ocean biomes rarely. Even rarer, they can generate above water, atop beaches, icebergs, ocean monuments or in ravines.

## Structure
Shipwrecks generate in multiple positions, either upright, on their side or entirely upside-down. They generate in various states of decay, in many time generating without the bow or stern of the ship. The entire ship is made of wood logs and planks, and up to 3 chests. Each section of the ship generates with chests, although if the section is missing, the chest will be as well.
_Drowned will spawn in groups of 1-8 in the shipwreck, with one group per chest the structure has._⁽¹⁾

## Loot
Shipwrecks contain up to 3 loot chests, which generate in different sections of the ship. If a section of the ship which has a chest is missing, the corresponding chest will also be missing. A supply chest generates in the bow of the ship, a treasure chest generates in the lower section of the stern, and a map chest generates in the upper section of the stern.
