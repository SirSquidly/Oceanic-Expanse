Coral reefs are dense, vibrant features of coral generating in the middle of [warm oceans](../generation/warm-ocean.md). [Tropical fish](../mobs/tropical-fish.md) and [Pufferfish](../mobs/pufferfish.md) spawn here.


## Generation
Coral reefs generate in the center of warm oceans, and are noise-based. They generate extremely dense with coral and coral fans attached to coral blocks. [Sea pickles](../blocks/sea-pickle.md) and Seagrass also generate in coral reefs, around or atop the larger coral features.

Larger coral features generate in 3 distinct styles, each with unique shapes and size.

**Coral Stalks** generate with a trunk, from which 2-4 branches generate pointing upwards.

**Coral Branches** generate much lower, with 2-3 branches pointed horizontally.

**Coral Bulbs** are spherical and hollow, with various holes perforating the surface.
