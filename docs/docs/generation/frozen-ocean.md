---
title: "Frozen Ocean"
---

Frozen oceans are large, icy regions of ocean with prominent icebergs.

## Generation
Like all ocean regions, [shipwrecks](../generation/shipwreck.md) and ocean monuments generate. [Kelp forests](../generation/kelp-forests.md) do not generate in frozen oceans.

### Icebergs
Large icebergs generate on the frozen ocean surface, made of packed ice, snow blocks, and uncommonly [blue ice](../blocks/blue-ice.md).

### Ice sheets
Ice sheets generate in _large, spread out_ patches of ice across frozen ocean surfaces.

### Seafloor
<img align="right" img src='https://i.imgur.com/ppufihY.png' width='400'>

Frozen ocean seafloors feature scattered patches of [sea stars](../blocks/sea-star.md), [dulse](../blocks/dulse.md), [tube sponge](../blocks/tube-sponge.md), and granite chunks. The seabed is only gravel
