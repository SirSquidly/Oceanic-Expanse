Tropical Fish are a type of passive fish that comes in 2,700 colorful variants.

## Spawning
Tropical Fish spawn underwater in Oceans, in groups of _1-8_⁽¹⁾. They can only spawn if any coral blocks are below them, making them common in [coral reefs](../generation/coral-reefs.md). They only spawn within 1 block above sea level (Y=63) and 12 blocks below sea level (Y=50).

## Drops
When killed, adult tropical fish drop:
* 1 Clownfish
* 1–3 Exp when killed by a player or tamed wolf.
* 1 Bonemeal (5% chance)

## Behavior
When outside of water, tropical fish flop about randomly. After 20 seconds outside of water, they will begin to suffocate.

### Breeding
Tropical fish can be bred using any type of vanilla seeds.

## Variants
Tropical fish come in many variants, with a combination of 2 shapes, 6 patterns, _15 colors_⁽²⁾ for the body, and _15 colors_⁽²⁾ for pattern. When spawning, 90% of tropical fish spawn within a set of 22 variants, while the rest are completely randomized. There are about 2,700 natural variants.

### Names
When in a bucket, the _bucket name_⁽²⁾ and _tooltip_⁽²⁾ will display the exact variant of tropical fish with a unique name. This name is constructed based on their colors, pattern, and shape, in the format of [body color]-[pattern color (if not same as body)] [Combo of pattern and shape]. This leads to combinations such as "Grey Kob", "Red-Green Blockfish", and "Light Blue-Purple Spotty".

_Natural tropical fish never use the color black._⁽²⁾

## Trivia
* Originally, only Clownfish were actually planned to be made in the mod, with a model and unique texture made very early in the mods development. The rest of tropical fish were later added, because they are cool.
