Salmon are a type of passive fish.

## Spawning
Salmon spawn underwater in groups of 1-5. In Rivers, they can only spawn in there are less than 5 salmon within 32 blocks, while in Oceans they can only spawn if any ice blocks are above them. They only spawn within 1 block above sea level (Y=63) and 12 blocks below sea level (Y=50).

Salmon spawn in 3 different sizes, 34% Small (NBT 'Size' 1), 51% Normal (NBT 'Size' 2), and 15% Large (NBT 'Size' 3). Using /summon, the player can spawn salmon of any size between 0-255.

## Drops
When killed, adult salmon drop:
* 1 Raw Salmon (1 Cooked Salmon if killed while on fire)
* 1–3 Exp when killed by a player or tamed wolf.
* 1 Bonemeal (5% chance)

## Behavior
When outside of water, salmon flop about randomly. After 20 seconds outside of water, they will begin to suffocate.

### Breeding
Salmon can be bred using any type of vanilla seeds.

### Expansion⁽²⁾
When feeding a Salmon using any breeding items, they will grow in size. This growth changes their hitbox and visual size, but not their health.

## Entity Data
* Size: The size of the Salmon and it's hitbox. This value is zero-based, so 0 is the smallest size.
