Tropical Slimes are an aquatic variant of Slime that spawns underwater. They have higher base stats compared to Normal Slimes, and are unaffected by Water.

## Spawning
Tropical Slimes spawn in two sizes, being medium or large. Large tropical slimes are very rare.

Tropical Slimes' size is affected by [regional difficulty](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Regional_difficulty), (CRD * 0.01)%, or a chance of 10% large at max difficulty.

### Slime Chunks
Tropical Slimes follow the same spawning rules as normal slimes, spawning in any [slime chunks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime#Slime_chunks), albeit only within Ocean Biomes, and underwater.

### Splitting
On Death, large tropical slimes will spawn 2-4 medium tropical slimes. Unlike normal slimes though, medium tropical slimes do not split.

### Jungles⁽¹⁾
Tropical Slimes can spawn within jungles, following the same rules as [slime spawn spawning](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime#Swamps), albeit only in water. Just like swamp slimes, their spawning is influenced by the phase of the moon.

## Drops
When killed, tropical slimes drop:
* 0-2 [Tropical Slimeball](../items/tropical-slimeball.md) (+1 per looting level)
* 1–3 Exp when killed by a player or tamed wolf.

Unlike normal slimes, any size of Tropical Slime has the same drops.

Along with this, a Tropical Slime will spawn 1 live [tropical fish](../mobs/tropical-fish.md).

## Behavior
Tropical Slimes are functionally very similar to slimes, with the exception that they do not require oxygen, and completely ignore the effects of water.

Tropical Slimes do not target any Players or Iron Golems that are not currently wet, either by being in water or rain. When not targeting any mob, they will not deal any collision damage.

A tropical slime's maximum health is equal to its size squared, multiplied by 2. This makes them more dangerous than normal slimes, as medium ones have 8 health, while large have 32. Their attack damage is the same as normal slimes, being equal to its size.

### Bucketing⁽¹⁾
Using an Empty Bucket on a Tropical Slime will kill it instantly, and give the player a random bucket of Tropical Fish. This does not spawn any of the Tropical Slime's Drops or Exp.

### Evaporating
When on Fire, or within the Nether, a Tropical Slime will be to emit Smoke particles. After 3 seconds, the tropical slime will evaporate, disappearing in a puff of smoke, and leaving behind its Tropical Fish.

## Entity Data
* Size: The size of the tropical Slime. The sizes that spawn naturally are either 1 or 4. This value is zero-based, so 0 is the smallest. Values that are greater than 126 get clamped to 126.
* wasOnGround: If the tropical slime is on the ground.
