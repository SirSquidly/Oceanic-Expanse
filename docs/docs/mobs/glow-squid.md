A glow squid is a bio-luminescent variant of the squid, typically in deep oceans or underground waters.

## Spawning
Glow squids spawn underwater in deep ocean biomes below _Y-40_⁽¹⁾, in groups of 2-4.

## Drops
When killed, glow squids drop:
* 0-2 [Glow ink sacs](../items/glow-ink-sac.md) (+1 per looting level)
* 1–3 Exp when killed by a player or tamed wolf.

## Behavior
Glow squid swim about aimlessly. They constantly emit shimmering particles.

When hurt, they stop glowing for 5 seconds, and gain a speed boost.

When outside of water, glow squids flop about randomly. After 20 seconds outside of water, they will begin to suffocate.
