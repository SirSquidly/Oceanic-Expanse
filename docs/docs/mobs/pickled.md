Pickled are a subvariant of the Zombie, being a variant of the [Drowned](../mobs/drowned.md), sharing many similarities.

## Spawning
Pickled do not spawn naturally yet. 

## Drops
When killed, pickled drop:
* 0-2 Rotten Flesh (+1 per looting level)
* 0-1 [Sea Pickle](../blocks/sea-pickle.md) (+1 per looting level)
* 5 Exp when killed by a player or tamed wolf.
* 1 Gold Ingot (5% chance, +1% per looting level)

## Behavior
<img align="right"  img src='https://i.imgur.com/p6FJqxI.gif' width='200'>

Pickled function identically to [drowned](../mobs/drowned.md), and thus very similarly to Zombies. They prefer traveling through water, not requiring oxygen, and moving faster than normal mobs in it.

When outside of water or rain, the glowing green intestines of the pickles retract, and the Pickled begins to shake.

### Swimming
When in water, Pickled will swim to match their target, and move in to attack. If it is day, they will keep their lower body submerged in order not to burn.

### Rick
If a Pickled is named `Rick` (using either a name tag or a renamed spawn egg/spawn bucket), it is re-textured with a white shirt, all the green glowing intestines are shifted to a more bluish-green, a blue unibrow is added, and a bit of dripping from the mouth.

This particular texture is based on the fictional character Rick Sanchez from the adult animated sci-fi sitcom series _Rick and Morty_, and specifically references the internet meme 'Pickle Rick', which held prominence in 2017.

## Trivia
* The Pickled is based off of the Minecraft [Coral Crafters Skin Pack](https://www.minecraft.net/it-it/article/new-aquatic-skins-out-now), specifically the [Sea Pickle Wanderer](https://www.minecraft.net/it-it/article/new-aquatic-skins-out-now) skin. The facial structure and placement of sea pickle parts are directly inspired by the skin.
