Cod are a type of passive fish.

## Spawning
Cod spawn underwater in any variant of Oceans, in groups of 3-7. They only spawn within 1 block above sea level (Y=63) and 12 blocks below sea level (Y=50).

## Drops
When killed, adult cod drop:
* 1 Raw Fish (1 Cooked Fish if killed while on fire)
* 1–3 Exp when killed by a player or tamed wolf.
* 1 Bonemeal (5% chance)

## Behavior
When outside of water, cod flop about randomly. After 20 seconds outside of water, they will begin to suffocate.

### Breeding
Cod can be bred using any type of vanilla seeds.
