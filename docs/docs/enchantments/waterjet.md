title: "Waterjet"
---

Waterjet is a [trident](../items/trident.md) enchantment that increases damage to wet mobs.

## Usage
Each level of Waterjet adds _1_⁽¹⁾ extra damage per hit.

Waterjet is only applied when the hit mob is wet: either by water or rain.

## Incompatibilities
Waterjet and [Imapling](../enchantments/impaling.md) are mutually exclusive, and cannot be combined.

## Mod Compatibility
_Main page: [Mod Compatibilities](../compatibility/mod-compatibilities.md)_  
### Enchantment Descriptions
The following description is attached to Waterjet when Enchantment Descriptions is installed:  
`Increases the damage to wet mobs.`
