---
title: "Conduit Power"
---

Conduit Power is a status effect that gives Water Breathing, Night Vision, and Haste to the affected entity when submerged in water.

## Effect
When an affected entity is submerged in water (head inside of water), they are given:
* Water Breathing
* Night Vision
* Haste

The level of Conduit Power(higher levels only possible through commands) directly affects the level of Haste given, but doesn't affect the other effects. 

All given effects are only active when the entity's head remains submerged, and are otherwise removed.

## Causes

| Cause     | Potency | Length | Notes | 
| --------- | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| [Activated Conduit](../blocks/conduit.md#activating) | I   | 0:10   | Reapplied every second within range. |
| Charm    | I   | 0:01   | Reapplied every second, at the cost of 1 durability each time. |

## Mod Compatibility
_Main page: [Mod Compatibilities](../compatibility/mod-compatibilities.md)_  
### Potion Descriptions
The following description is attached to Conduit Power when Potion Descriptions is installed:  
`Grants Water Breathing, Night Vision, and Haste when submerged in water.`
