A turtle shell is a rare armor piece that applies Water Breathing when worn outside of water.

## Obtaining
### Crafting
A turtle shell is crafted from 5 [scute](../items/scute.md).

## Usage
A turtle shell is equipped in the top armor slot like any other helmet.

### Defense and Durability
Turtle shells give +2 defense points when worn, and have 275 durability.

### Turtle Shell effect
Equipping a turtle shell applies the Water Breathing status effect for 10 seconds, allowing them to stay underwater for longer. This effect is reapplied constantly while exposed to air, and will count down when underwater.

### Brewing Ingredient
A turtle shell can be used to brew a [potion of the Turtle Master](../items/potions.md#potion-of-the-turtle-master) from an awkward potion.
