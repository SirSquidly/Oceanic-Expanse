Crab Bisque is a food item.

## Obtaining
### Crafting
Crab Bisque can be crafted using a [Cooked Crab](../items/cooked-crab.md), a [Crustacean Shell](../items/crustacean-shell.md), a Milk Bucket, and a Bowl.

## Usage
### Food
Crab Bisque restores 10 hunger and 16 saturation.

The bowl is returned to the player after being eaten, identically to the vanilla soups and stews.

_It also removes any status effects a Milk Bucket would._⁽¹⁾
