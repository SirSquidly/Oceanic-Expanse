A heart of the sea is a rare item used in crafting a [conduit](../blocks/conduit.md).

## Obtaining
### Chest Loot
A heart of the sea is exclusively found within [shipwrecks](../generation/shipwreck.md), with a 20% chance to spawn in the Treasure Chest.

| Item        | Structure | Container | Stack Size | Chance |
| ----------- | --------- | --------- | -------- | ------ |
| Heart of the Sea | [Shipwreck](../generation/shipwreck.md) | Chest    | 1   | 20%    |

## Usage
### Crafting
A heart of the sea and 8 [nautilus shells](../items/nautilus-shell.md) can be used to craft a [conduit](../blocks/conduit.md).
