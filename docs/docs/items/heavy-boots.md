Heavy Boots are a rare armor piece that applies [Descent](../effects/descent.md) when worn.

## Obtaining
### Chest Loot
Heavy boots are exclusively found within [shipwrecks](../generation/shipwreck.md), with a 20% chance to spawn in the Supply Chest. They never spawn with enchantments.

| Item        | Structure | Container | Stack Size | Chance |
| ----------- | --------- | --------- | -------- | ------ |
| Heavy Boots | [Shipwreck](../generation/shipwreck.md) | Chest    | 1   | 20%    |

### Repairing
Heavy boots can be repaired in an Anvil with [barnacle shells](../items/barnacle-shells.md), preserving the enchantments and restoring 20% of the max durability. They can also be repaired by crafting two together, although this removes any enchantments.

## Usage
Heavy boots are equipped in the bottom armor slot like any other boots.

### Defense and Durability
Heavy boots give +2 defense points when worn, and have 325 durability.

### Heavy Boots effect
Equipping heavy boots applies the [Descent](../effects/descent.md) status effect for 10 seconds, adding extra gravity to the player. This effect is reapplied constantly while the heavy boots are worn.

## Trivia
* The heavy boots are intended to be used for quick deep diving, and were considered to be left out of the mod. Adding them in though, they proved to be very fun to just jump about with, so they are left in.
