A Raw Lobster is a food item that can be eaten or cooked into a [Cooked Lobster](../items/cooked-lobster.md).


## Obtaining
### Mob Drops
#### Lobsters
Adult [lobsters](../mobs/lobster.md) drop 1 raw lobster when killed. This amount increases depending on the lobster's size, being `size / 3`, rounded down. Along with this, each level of Looting adds 0-1 more raw lobster. 

For example, a size 10 lobster killed with Looting 3 will drop 3-6 Raw Lobster.

## Usage
### Food
Raw Lobster restores 3 hunger and 0.9 saturation.

### Smelting Ingredient
Raw Lobster can be smelted to produce [Cooked Lobster](../items/cooked-lobster.md).

### Lobsters
Raw Lobster can be used to lead [Lobsters](../mobs/lobster.md), and can be given to or collected by Lobsters to [eat](../mobs/lobster.md#eating).
