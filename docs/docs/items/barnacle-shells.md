Barnacle shells are an item obtained from [shelly sand](../blocks/shelly-sand.md). It is used in various recipes.

## Obtaining
### Shelly Sand
Barnacle shells have a chance of being gathered from Shelly sand by either using a shovel on, or directly digging, the block.

## Usage
### Crafting
Barnacle shells can be used to craft [coquina](../blocks/coquina.md).

### Repairing
Barnacle shells can be used in an anvil to repair heavy boots, restoring 25% of the total durability while preserving the enchantments.

### Brewing Ingredient
Barnacle shells can be used to brew a [potion of descent](../items/potions.md#potion-of-descent) from an awkward potion.


## Mod Compatibility
_Main page: [Mod Compatibilities](../compatibility/mod-compatibilities.md)_  
### Just Enough Items (JEI)
The following information is attached to Barnacle Shells when JEI is installed:

`  `  
`Can also be used to repair Heavy Boots in an anvil.`
