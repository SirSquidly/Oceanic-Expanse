A Tropical Slimeball is a crafting ingredient and food item dropped by Tropical Slimes.

## Obtaining
### Mob Drops
Tropical Slimes of size 2 or 1 will drop 0-2 tropical slimeballs when killed. Each level of looting increases the maximum number dropped by 1.

### Crafting
Tropical Slimeballs can be crafted from 1 [Tropical Slime Block](../blocks/tropical-slime-block.md).

## Usage
### Food
Tropical slimeballs remove 3 hunger, and restore 4 air bubbles. Saturation is not affected.

Unlike most foods, tropical slimeballs can always be eaten.

They are eaten at double the speed of normal foods.

### Crafting Ingredient
9 tropical slimeballs can be crafted into a [tropical slime block](../blocks/tropical-slime-block.md).
