Lobster Bisque is a food item.

## Obtaining
### Crafting
Lobster Bisque can be crafted using a [Cooked Lobster](../items/cooked-lobster.md), a [Crustacean Shell](../items/crustacean-shell.md), a Milk Bucket, and a Bowl.

## Usage
### Food
Lobster Bisque restores 14 hunger and 22.4 saturation. This gives it the highest hunger and saturation out of any food in vanilla minecraft.

The bowl is returned to the player after being eaten, identically to the vanilla soups and stews.

_It also removes any status effects a Milk Bucket would._⁽¹⁾
