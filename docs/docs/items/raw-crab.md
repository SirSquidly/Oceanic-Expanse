A Raw Crab is a food item that can be eaten or cooked into a [Cooked Crab](../items/cooked-crab.md).


## Obtaining
### Mob Drops
#### Crabs
Adult [Crabs](../mobs/crab.md) drop 1 raw lobster when killed. Each level of Looting adds 0-1 more raw crab. 

## Usage
### Food
Raw Crab restores 2 hunger and 1.6 saturation.

### Smelting Ingredient
Raw Crab can be smelted to produce [Cooked Crab](../items/cooked-crab.md).

### Lobsters
Raw Crab can be used to lead [Lobsters](../mobs/lobster.md), and can be given to or collected by Lobsters to [eat](../mobs/lobster.md#eating).
