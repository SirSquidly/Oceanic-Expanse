Dried Kelp is a food item that can quickly be eaten by the player. It can be crafted into [dried kelp blocks](../blocks/dried-kelp-block.md).

## Obtaining
### Smelting
[Kelp](../blocks/kelp.md) can be cooked in a furnace to create dried kelp, and creates 0.1 experience.

### Crafting
A dried kelp block can be uncrafted into dried kelp.

## Usage
### Food
Dried kelp restores 1 hunger and 0.6 saturation, and is eaten at double the speed of normal foods.

### Crafting Ingredient
9 dried dulse can be crafted into a [dried kelp block](../blocks/dried-kelp-block.md).
