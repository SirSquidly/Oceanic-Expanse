Cooked Crab is a food item that can be eaten or crafted into [Crab Bisque](../items/crab-bisque.md).


## Obtaining
### Mob Drops
#### Crabs
Adult [Crabs](../mobs/crab.md) drop 1 cooked crab when killed while on fire. Each level of Looting adds 0-1 more cooked crab. 

### Cooking
[Raw Crab](../items/raw-crab.md) can be smelted to produce cooked crab.

## Usage
### Food
Cooked Crab restores 6 hunger and 7.2 saturation.

### Crafting Ingredient
A Cooked Crab, a [Crustacean Shell](../items/crustacean-shell.md), a Milk Bucket, and a Bowl can be used to craft [Crab Bisque](../items/crab-bisque.md).

### Lobsters
Cooked Crab can be used to lead [Lobsters](../mobs/lobster.md), and can be given to or collected by Lobsters to [eat](../mobs/lobster.md#eating).
