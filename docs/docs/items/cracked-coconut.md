Cracked Coconut is a food item.

## Obtaining
### Block Loot
Cracked Coconuts can be obtained by mining a [Coconut](../blocks/coconut.md) using any tool or sword (beside a hoe). A coconut drops 2 cracked coconuts.

Alternatively, a coconut falling over 6 blocks will break into 2 cracked coconuts when landing.

## Usage
### Food
Cracked Coconuts restores 3 hunger and 3.6 saturation.

### Crafting Ingredient
Crafting a Cracked coconut with a bucket produces a bucket of milk.
