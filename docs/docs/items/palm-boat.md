A Palm Boat is a palm variant of [boat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat), functioning identically to other vanilla boats.

## Obtaining
Repeatedly hitting a palm boat will make it drop as an item. Tools and weapons that deal more than 4 damage will make the boat drop in one hit.

### Crafting
Palm Boats can be crafted using 5 [Palm Planks](../blocks/palm-planks.md).

## Usage
Palm Boats share the same uses, properties, and rotation as vanilla boats.
