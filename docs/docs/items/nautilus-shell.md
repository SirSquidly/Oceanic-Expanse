A nautilus shell is an item used in crafting a [conduit](../blocks/conduit.md).

## Obtaining
### Mob Drops
_3%_⁽¹⁾ of [drowned](../mobs/drowned.md) spawn with a nautilus shell in their offhand, and will always drop it.

### Shelly Sand
Nautilus shells have a rare chance of being gathered from [shelly sand](../blocks/shelly-sand.md) by either using a shovel on, or directly digging, the block.

## Usage
### Crafting
A [heart of the sea](../items/heart-of-the-sea.md) and 8 nautilus shells can be used to craft a [conduit](../blocks/conduit.md).
