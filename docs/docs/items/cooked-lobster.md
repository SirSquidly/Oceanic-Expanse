A Cooked Lobster is a food item that can be eaten or crafted into [Lobster Bisque](../items/lobster-bisque.md).


## Obtaining
### Mob Drops
#### Lobsters
Adult [lobsters](../mobs/lobster.md) drop 1 cooked lobster when killed while on fire. This amount increases depending on the lobster's size, being `size / 3`, rounded down. Along with this, each level of Looting adds 0-1 more raw lobster. 

For example, a size 10 lobster killed with Looting 3, while on fire, will drop 3-6 Cooked Lobster.

### Cooking
[Raw Lobster](../items/raw-lobster.md) can be smelted to produce cooked lobster.

## Usage
### Food
Cooked Lobster restores 8 hunger and 12.8 saturation.

### Crafting Ingredient
A Cooked Lobster, a [Crustacean Shell](../items/crustacean-shell.md), a Milk Bucket, and a Bowl can be used to craft [Lobster Bisque](../items/lobster-bisque.md).

### Lobsters
Cooked Lobster can be used to lead [Lobsters](../mobs/lobster.md), and can be given to or collected by Lobsters to [eat](../mobs/lobster.md#eating).
