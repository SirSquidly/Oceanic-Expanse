A Conduit Charm is a tool that grants [Conduit Power](../effects/conduit-power.md) within a _16_⁽¹⁾ block radius, when held underwater.

## Obtaining
### Crafting
Conduit Charms can be crafted using a Pearl, 2 [Nautilus Shells](../items/nautilus-shell.md), and 5 Iron Nuggets.

## Usage
If a player is holding a conduit charm in their main-hand or off-hand slot and submerges their head in water, the conduit charm activates. The conduit charm does not activate if the holder already has Conduit Power, such as directly given from an active [Conduit](../blocks/conduit.md).

### Effect
Similar to an active conduit, the conduit charm works in pulses. Each pulse grants Conduit Power to its holder _and all players within 16 blocks_⁽¹⁾ for 5 seconds. Conduit particles pull toward the holder and _a sound plays_⁽¹⁾ each time this activates.

Each time Conduit Power is applied, 1 durability is lost from the conduit charm. Conduit Charms only have _30_⁽¹⁾ durability, effectively making the item last _2.5 minutes_⁽¹⁾ of constant usage.

### Enchantments
Despite having durability like many tools, the conduit charm will reject Mending or Unbreaking.
