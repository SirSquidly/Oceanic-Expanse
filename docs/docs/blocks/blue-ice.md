Blue ice is a block variant of packed ice, but more slippery.

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Blue ice can only be obtained using a Silk Touch enchanted tool, although a pickaxe is the fastest. If mined without, nothing is dropped.

### Natural Generation
Blue ice generates as parts of chunks attached to the underside of icebergs within [frozen oceans](../generation/frozen-ocean.md). 

### Crafting
9 packed ice can be crafted into blue ice.

## Usage
Blue ice is more slippery (_0.989_⁽¹⁾) compared to other variants of ice, allowing for faster item transport.

Blue ice is completely solid, allowing any blocks to be placed on it. It also cannot melt.

Blue ice is not a storage block, and cannot be uncrafted.

### Light⁽²⁾
Blue ice can emit light, as specified in the config.
