---
title: "Palm Sapling"
---

Palm saplings are non-solid blocks that grow into [palm trees](../generation/palm-tree.md).

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Palm saplings can be mined instantly by hand or any tool.

### Drops
[Crabs](../mobs/crab.md) holding a palm sapling will drop it upon death. Alternatively, the player can drop or right-click a food item on the crab to attempt a barter. Bartering will only have a 50% chance to succeed.

## Usage
### Growing Trees
Palm saplings can be planted on any blocks of a sand or dirt material, such as gravel or grass blocks. Using Bonemeal, the sapling can grow into a  [palm tree](../generation/palm-tree.md).
