Palm Planks are crafted from [Palm Logs](../blocks/palm-log.md) or [Palm Wood](../blocks/palm-wood.md), and can be made into Slabs, Stairs, Fences, Fence Gates, and Doors.

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Palm Planks can be mined by hand, but an axe speeds up the process.

## Usage
### Crafting Ingredient
Palm Planks can be crafted into Slabs, Stairs, Fences, Fence Gates, and Doors.
