Guardian Spikes are a rare block used in crafting [tridents](../items/trident.md).

## Obtaining
### Breaking
A Guardian Spike requires a pickaxe to be picked up.

### Mob Drops
Guardian Spikes are a rare drop for Guardians, with a _1% chance_⁽¹⁾ (_+0.5% per Looting Level_⁽¹⁾) to drop.

Elder Guardians have a _100% chance_⁽¹⁾ (_unaffected by Looting_⁽¹⁾) to drop a Guardian Spike.

## Usage
Falling onto Guardian Spikes multiplies fall damage by _1.5_⁽¹⁾. Mobs immune to fall damage are unaffected.

### Crafting Ingredient
Guardian Spikes can be crafted with Prismarine Shards to create a [trident](../items/trident.md).


## Mod Compatibility
_Main page: [Mod Compatibilities](../compatibility/mod-compatibilities.md)_  
### Just Enough Items (JEI)
The following information is attached to Guardian Spikes when JEI is installed:  
`Guardian Spikes are primarily used to craft Tridents. They are either dropped by an Elder Guardian, or very rarely dropped from a normal Guardian.`  
`  `  
`Falling onto a Guardian Spike multiplies fall damage by 2.`
