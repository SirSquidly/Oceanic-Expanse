A coconut is a block affected by gravity. They grow from [flowering coconut leaves](../blocks/flowering-coconut-leaves.md) on a [palm tree](../generation/palm-tree.md).

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Coconuts can be mined by hand. Using any tool or sword (beside a hoe) will result in it dropping two [cracked coconuts](../items/cracked-coconut.md), with an Axe being the fastest. If mined when not at full age, it drops nothing.

### Flowering coconut leaves
Every random game tick there is a 20% chance for [flowering coconut leaves](../blocks/flowering-coconut-leaves.md) to spawn a coconut below it, as long as the space below is only occupied by air.

### Drops
[Crabs](../mobs/crab.md) holding a coconut will drop it upon death. Alternatively, the player can drop or right-click a food item on the crab to attempt a barter. Bartering will only have a 50% chance to succeed.

## Usage
Coconuts grown from [flowering coconut leaves](../blocks/flowering-coconut-leaves.md) are in a hanging state, and will grow overtime. Bonemeal can be used to speed the growth of a hanging coconut up. When a coconut is at full maturity, it will fall on it's next random tick.

A coconut placed by the player requires a supporting block below it, or it will fall to the next available block.

### Falling coconuts
A fully aged coconut will either at a random tick, or if there is not supporting block above it, fall in the same way anvils or sand does. Coconuts make a comedic clonk sound when landing on an entity⁽¹⁾.

A falling coconut damages any player, mod, or item it falls on. This damage is proportional to the fall distance: each block after the first adds 1 damage⁽¹⁾, with a cap of 19 damage⁽¹⁾. Just like the anvil, wearing a helmet reduces the damage by 1⁄4, at the cost of durability on the helmet.

A coconut falling from a height greater than or equal to 6 blocks⁽¹⁾ will instead break upon touching the ground, dropping two cracked coconuts.
