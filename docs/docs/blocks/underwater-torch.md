An underwater torch is a non-solid block that emits light underwater.

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Underwater Torches can be mined and collected instantly

### Crafting
Underwater Torches can be crafted from a [glow ink sac](../items/glow-ink-sac.md) and Iron Ingot.

## Usage
<img align="right" img src='https://i.imgur.com/8X0UZkB.gif' width='300'>
An Underwater Torch can be placed on top or on the sides of most solid blocks, exactly like a normal torch. Unlike normal torches, Underwater torches can be placed and operate underwater.

### Light
Underwater Torches emit a light level of _14_⁽¹⁾.
