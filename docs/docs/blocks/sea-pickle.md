Sea Pickles are light-emitting colonies of stationary marine animals.

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Sea pickles can be mined instantly by hand. Each colony drops as many pickles as are in the block (so a colony of 3 drops 3 pickles).

### Drops
[Pickled](../mobs/pickled.md) drop from 0 to 2 sea pickles. Each level of looting increases the max drop by 1.

### Natural Generation
Sea pickles generate in [warm oceans](../generation/warm-ocean.md) and [coral reefs](../generation/coral-reefs.md), in patches on the seafloor.

## Usage
Sea pickles can be placed atop most blocks, up to four per block.
Underwater, Sea Pickles produce light, with one pickle producing a light level of 6, +3 per each additional pickle (so  all four pickles produce a light level of 15).

Using Bonemeal on sea pickle(s) increases the amount in a colony by 1 - 3 pickles, to a max of 4. If the colony is atop a coral block, they can randomly spread to neighboring empty spaces atop coral blocks, as long as there is a block of water above aswell.

### Smelting Ingredient
Sea pickles can be smelted to produce lime dye.

## Mod Compatibility
_Main page: [Mod Compatibilities](../compatibility/mod-compatibilities.md)_  
### Just Enough Items (JEI)
The following information is attached to Sea Pickles when JEI is installed:  
`Sea Pickles can be placed into groups up to 4. When underwater, they emit light.`  
`  `  
`Using Bonemeal on a colony of Sea Pickles less than 4 will increase the number. If the colony is atop a Coral Block, Sea Pickles can be spread atop nearby Coral Blocks.`
