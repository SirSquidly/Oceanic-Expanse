Kelp is a seaweed that can be planted underwater, or cooked to make [dried kelp](../items/dried-kelp.md).

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Kelp can be mined by hand. Each growth stage of Dulse adds an additional piece when mined. 

### Natural Generation
Kelp generates in [kelp forests](../generation/kelp-forests.md) across most ocean biomes.

## Usage
Kelp can be placed underwater, atop any solid block. When placed by hand, kelp is given a random age between 0 and 14.

### Growth Mechanics
Kelp does not require any light or sky access to grow. Whenever kelp grows, it will extend upwards by 1 block, and its age will increase by 1. At age 15, it stops growing.

Bone meal used anywhere on a kelp stalk will grow the stalk by 1 block, if the top is not at max age.

When a kelp block is broken, the age of the kelp block below it will be randomized between 0 and 14, thus continuing to grow.

Using Shears on the top of Kelp will lock it to it's current age.

### Smelting Ingredient
Kelp can be smelted to cook [dried kelp](../items/dried-kelp.md).
