A Tropical Slime Block is a storage block made of [Tropical Slimeballs](../items/tropical-slimeball.md). It has sticky properties to other blocks, while ignoring sticky pistons, and allowing fast entites to pass through.

## Obtaining

### Breaking
Tropical Slime Blocks can be mined instantly by hand.

### Crafting
Tropical Slime Blocks can be crafted using 9 [Tropical Slimeballs](../items/tropical-slimeball.md).

## Usage
Tropical Slime Blocks slow movement atop them.

### Collision
A player or mob moving at a high enough velocity will not collide with a tropical slime block, instead passing right through it. 

Entities that are mounted or being ridden will never pass through.

Tropical Slimes always ignore the collision of tropical slime blocks.

### Pistons
Tropical Slime Blocks share most of the same mechanics as Slime Blocks when interacted with using pistons, except they cannot be pulled directly by sticky pistons.

### Crafting Ingredient
A tropical slime block can be crafted into 9 tropical slimeballs.
