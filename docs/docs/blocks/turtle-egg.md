A turtle egg is a block containing a cluster of 1-4 eggs, which hatch into baby [turtle](../mobs/turtle.md).

## Obtaining
### Breaking
Turtle eggs can only be obtained using Silk Touch enchanted tools. If mined without, nothing is dropped. The hatch stage is reset when mined.

### Post-generation
After two turtles are bred using seagrass, one will travel back to their home beach, dig in the sand, and place 1-4 turtle eggs.

## Usage
Turtle eggs can be placed atop most blocks, up to four per block. They do not require a support block, and are not affected by gravity.

### Trampling⁽¹⁾
When any living entity stands atop or jumps on turtle eggs, there is a chance of one of the eggs breaking.

Zombie-type mobs (such as Zombies, Zombie Pigmen, [Drowned](../mobs/drowned.md), ect) will seek out and trample turtle eggs. They will only go to turtle eggs if there are 2 blocks of air above them, and it is within a 45x7x45 area. After reaching the egg, the zombie mob will jump repeatedly on the egg, trampling _1 egg_⁽¹⁾ every ~4 seconds.

[Turtles](../mobs/drowned.md) cannot trample or break turtle eggs.

### Hatching
If turtle eggs are atop a sand block⁽²⁾, they will slowly hatch overtime. Hatching is displayed as visible cracks on the turtle eggs, and they will hatch after cracking 3 times.

Each random tick, there is a 1/500 chance of an egg cracking. During night, between 21600 and 22550 ticks in-game time (3:36 am and 4:33 am), each random tick is a guaranteed crack. This means eggs will crack far faster within this time.

When a crack occurs, all eggs in the block crack simultaneously.
